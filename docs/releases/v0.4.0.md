evidencestore is an independent witness for logs. A collector next to the log sends every line to a store on another machine, and the store can later prove any of those lines to an auditor, offline and without being trusted: the exact bytes, their place in a hash chain, a signed chain head and RFC 3161 time-stamps from before and after the record. Retention works by destroying a month's key: the bodies become unreadable in every copy, while the chain and the proofs stay valid.

This release changes what the store's artifacts show and how a chain head is made. It reads nothing written by v0.3.1 or earlier: see Compatibility before you upgrade.

## New in this release

- **A single-record bundle no longer shows other records.** To check the collector's promise, a single-record bundle carries every leaf from the head the earlier time-stamp certifies to the record's link. Until now those leaves were whole, so the bundle named the sources, record numbers and receive times of everything the store took in meanwhile. Now only time-stamps are whole and every other leaf is just its hash; each leaf enters the Merkle tree of its link with a flag that says whether it is a time-stamp, so a time-stamp shown by its hash breaks its link's root and cannot be hidden. What still shows is the pace of the whole store: how many leaves each link holds and when it was made ([Bundles](https://github.com/Nikolay1994Kaz/evidencestore/blob/v0.4.0/docs/bundles.md)). The bundle is smaller too: up to about 165 KB with one record a second and a time-stamp every 10 minutes.
- **The remote copy is encrypted.** Chunks of the evidence log and the chain go to `replica.url` encrypted with `copy.key`, a random key that `init` creates and that must reach `keys.backup_dir` before anything is copied. Whoever holds the copy sees chunk sizes, when they arrive and the signed heads — how fast the store grows — but no source names, records or receive times. A chunk moved under another name, offset or generation does not open. `restore` takes the key from `keys.backup_dir` (or `-copy-key FILE`) ([Copy, restore and the witness](https://github.com/Nikolay1994Kaz/evidencestore/blob/v0.4.0/docs/copy-and-witness.md)).
- **A chain head is a checkpoint over a Merkle tree of the links.** A head is now the number of links and the root of an RFC 6962 tree over all of them, signed in the [C2SP checkpoint](https://c2sp.org/tlog-checkpoint) format (the signature is also a valid C2SP signed note). TSA time-stamps certify the same number and root. Every bundle carries the frontier of that tree before its first link, at most one hash per bit of the link number, and `esverify` checks the signed head, the time-stamps and a witness's heads against the roots it computes. Whether one head extends another is shown by a few hashes, not by every link between them. The witness, the copy and `evidencestore statement` carry checkpoints. The store keeps the tree's hashes in `data_dir/tree.hashes`, a file derived from the chain and checked against it on every start.
- **Collectors are witnesses of the chain.** The store's answers to a collector carry its signed head and a proof of a few hashes that this head extends the one the collector accepted last. The collector writes the last head it accepted to `checkpoints.jsonl`, with the time by its own clock, at most every `checkpoint_every` (10 minutes by default) and when it stops, and a head that does not extend the last one goes, together with that one, to `checkpoints-conflict.jsonl`, with an error in its log; records keep being delivered. `esverify -heads DIR` reads these files as they are. When the store and the sources are run by different people, a rewritten chain shows at the collectors without any third party ([Collectors as witnesses](https://github.com/Nikolay1994Kaz/evidencestore/blob/v0.4.0/docs/copy-and-witness.md#collectors-as-witnesses)).
- **Restore checks the head against its name in the copy**: a signed head put under another number no longer stops `restore` with a crash or takes it to an older head.

## Compatibility

- This release reads nothing written by v0.3.1 or earlier: the Merkle trees of the links and the heads are built differently. The store refuses an older `data_dir` and says why, and `esverify` refuses older bundles by version (single-record bundles are now version 4, window and one-source bundles version 3) and names the release whose `esverify` reads them. Set the store up as a new installation: a new `data_dir`, new key directories and a new location for the copy and for the witness, since the new chain starts again from link 1. Keep the old installation's `esverify` for its bundles.
- `replica.url` now needs `keys.backup_dir`: a copy whose key is lost with `data_dir` cannot be restored. `keys.backup_dir` receives `copy.key` next to `keyring.dat` and `sign.key`, and `init` refuses to run where it already holds one of them.
- Collectors start over too, since the new store has a new key and knows none of their numbers: let each deliver what it holds to the old store, then give it an empty `spool_dir`, the new store's public key as `store_key` and this release's `escollect`, which keeps the store's heads as a witness. Without `from_start` it reads from the end of the log; with it, the whole log goes to the new store again.
- New dependency: `golang.org/x/mod` (the tree and its proofs, from the Go checksum database).

## Known issue

- `esverify -heads` takes the first head at or after the record's link by number and does not read when the witness received it. Whoever holds both `sign.key` and the witness credentials can sign a head later and add it to a witness bucket under an unused number, and `esverify` takes that head instead. Until it reads receive times, check the head it names yourself, as [Witness of the chain heads](https://github.com/Nikolay1994Kaz/evidencestore/blob/v0.4.0/docs/copy-and-witness.md#witness-of-the-chain-heads) describes; the same holds for v0.2.0 to v0.3.1. A collector's head journal is not open to this: the store cannot put a head there.

## Install

Binaries: `evidencestore` and `escollect` for Linux amd64 and arm64, `esverify` also for macOS (amd64, arm64) and Windows (amd64). Check them before use:

```sh
sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS --ignore-missing
gh attestation verify esverify-linux-amd64 --repo Nikolay1994Kaz/evidencestore
```

With Go 1.25.4 or newer: `go install github.com/Nikolay1994Kaz/evidencestore/cmd/esverify@v0.4.0`, likewise `cmd/evidencestore` and `cmd/escollect`.

Setting up a store and collectors, with systemd unit files: [README, Install](https://github.com/Nikolay1994Kaz/evidencestore#install).

## How it was tested

- The test gate with the race detector, in CI on Go 1.25.4.
- Mutation testing: 650 injected bugs on this release's code, 1–2 November, each caught by the tests (four only because the tests run out of time). In the first run two survived and one did not compile: one test saw the check it guards only through the tree over the links, another had lost its premise, and one mutation no longer fitted the code; the tests are rewritten and the mutation fixed (P-38 and P-39 in [`docs/dev/FINDINGS.md`](https://github.com/Nikolay1994Kaz/evidencestore/blob/v0.4.0/docs/dev/FINDINGS.md), in Russian). The last fix of the collector (P-42, P-43) came after the full run: the five mutations it added or rewrote are caught, and 39 of the collector's other 106 mutations, run again on the final code, are caught; the rest were not run again.
- Simulations on this release's code, no failures: a dishonest store owner, 2,000 scenarios (each of the 632 window bundles that left a record out was exposed by that record's promise); the receive-time clock, 1,000 scenarios; the store with crashes, 2,000 scenarios of 300 steps (37,294 crashes, 6,735 restores from the copy, 1,396 disks that lied about fsync); the collector, 2,000 scenarios in each of four worlds and 150 per world against the real store, in which no collector found a head that did not extend the last one it had accepted (after the last fix of the collector, 500 per world and 150 against the real store again).
- The demo passes. A test runs the real store with a collector and verifies a record's bundle against the collector's head journal, read the way `esverify -heads` reads it.
- The code of this release went through a review round by AI agents (verification of bundles, the store, the collector and the documentation) and two more passes over the fixes, the last of which found nothing critical; the findings and their fixes are in [`docs/dev/FINDINGS.md`](https://github.com/Nikolay1994Kaz/evidencestore/blob/v0.4.0/docs/dev/FINDINGS.md), in Russian.

Details: [README, How it was tested](https://github.com/Nikolay1994Kaz/evidencestore#how-it-was-tested).

## Before you use it

- Until 1.0 the on-disk formats (evidence log, chain, keyring) may change; each release will describe the way over.
- The pilot cryptography is not certified: AES-256-GCM from the Go standard library, root keys in files. The root store is a plug-in point for an HSM.
- Read [Known limitations](https://github.com/Nikolay1994Kaz/evidencestore#known-limitations).
- There is no container image yet.
